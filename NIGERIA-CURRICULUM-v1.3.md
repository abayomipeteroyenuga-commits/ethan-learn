# ETHAN Learn v1.3 — Nigeria Curriculum Research and Implementation

This build prioritizes Nigerian learners from Primary 1 through Senior Secondary 3.

## Official structure used
- NERDC's current Basic Education offering identifies English Studies and Mathematics throughout Primary/JSS, Basic Science in Primary 1–3, Basic Science & Technology and Basic Digital Literacy in Primary 4–6, and Intermediate Science, Digital Technologies and Business Studies in JSS.
- NERDC's current Senior Secondary offering lists English Language, General Mathematics, one trade subject, Citizenship and Heritage Studies, and Digital Technologies as core/compulsory. Biology, Chemistry and Physics are in the Science field; Commerce is in the Business field.
- Detailed topic progression was informed by the NERDC 9-Year BEC curriculum structure and organized into original ETHAN Learn teaching lessons; it does not reproduce the official curriculum text verbatim.

## Implemented
- 60 Nigerian class/subject courses
- 720 taught/practice lessons
- Primary 1–6, JSS 1–3, SS 1–3
- Dedicated Nigeria Curriculum navigation page
- Nigeria class badges on course cards
- All lessons include guided practice, mastery checks and quizzes
- Existing PWA, progress, certificates, notes, bookmarks and tutor interface preserved

## Important
This is an independent educational pathway. NERDC, WAEC and NECO do not endorse this application. Learners preparing for a specific examination should check the latest official examination syllabus/specification for that year.
