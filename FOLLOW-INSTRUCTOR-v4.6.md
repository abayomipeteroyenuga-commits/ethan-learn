# ETHAN Learn v4.6 — Follow Instructor

Lesson narration now follows the visible lesson content.

- Current spoken block is highlighted.
- Page smoothly scrolls the active block into the center.
- Mathematics/accounting workings stay grouped by semantic blocks rather than racing word-by-word.
- Follow Instructor can be turned ON/OFF from the lesson audio panel.
- Pause pauses speech and keeps the current block.
- Resume continues from the current block.
- Stop clears the active highlight.
- Manual wheel/touch/pointer interaction temporarily suspends auto-follow for about 8 seconds, so learners can inspect earlier content without the page fighting them.
- The preference is saved locally.
- Reduced-motion browser preferences are respected.

This uses browser speechSynthesis and does not require microphone permission.
