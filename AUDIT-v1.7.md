# ETHAN Learn v1.7 — Full Content Method

The course model now applies a common full-content foundation to every course:
definition and meaning; scope and purpose; background/development; importance;
essential terminology; concepts; principles/rules; methods and worked reasoning;
applications; guided practice; independent practice; common errors; ethics/quality;
mastery review and assessment.

Accounting receives an additional deep course pack covering:
nature/objectives/branches; concepts; accounting equation; double entry; source
documents; journals; ledgers; trial balance; errors; adjustments; depreciation;
financial statements; cash flow; financial analysis; internal control; bank
reconciliation; ethics; technology and a capstone case.

The enrichment is additive: Nigeria curriculum, global curriculum, 125 Digital
Courses, Tutor AI architecture, cloud sync, Voice and PWA features are retained.
