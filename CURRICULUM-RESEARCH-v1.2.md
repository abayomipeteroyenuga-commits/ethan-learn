# ETHAN Learn v1.2 — Global Curriculum Research

Implemented as a comparative curriculum rather than a reproduction of any copyrighted syllabus.

Research anchors used for scope decisions include:
- Ghana NaCCA SHS Mathematics and English curriculum architecture and WAEC Ghana WASSCE subject structure.
- South Africa Department of Basic Education CAPS/NCS subject structure for Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences and Business Studies.
- Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics and Chemistry syllabus structures.
- International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme Mathematics, Sciences and Individuals & Societies subject group structure.
- US Common Core English Language Arts and NGSS high-school physical/life science domains.
- India CBSE 2026–27 secondary/senior-secondary curriculum and Mathematics course structure.
- Australian Curriculum learning areas and senior secondary framework.
- New Zealand Curriculum/NCEA subject progression.
- Brazil BNCC upper-secondary areas of Languages, Mathematics and Natural Sciences.

The product labels this as comparative alignment and does not claim official endorsement or equivalence.
