# ETHAN Learn v4.3 — Deep Teaching Standard

Every runtime lesson is enriched with a learn-by-doing teaching layer:

1. Explain the idea
2. Show a worked/model example
3. Guided practice
4. Independent questions/tasks
5. Real-life/practical application
6. Check answers/model points where appropriate
7. Reflect, verify and improve

Mathematics receives calculation-focused practice. Simultaneous Equations has a dedicated full teaching lesson with elimination, substitution, three worked examples, guided questions, six independent questions, answers, exam technique and a real-life ticket problem.

Science receives safe investigation/data-analysis work. Accounting/finance receives transaction, calculation and control practice. Digital courses receive build-test-debug-improve work. Business receives case/decision/KPI work. Literature uses original or teacher/public-domain text analysis rather than copyrighted excerpts.

This layer enriches the existing curriculum rather than replacing it.
