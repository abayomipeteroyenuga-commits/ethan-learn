# ETHAN Learn v3.2 — Voice Fix + Comprehensive Topic Coverage

## Voice/listen fix
- Restored real lesson text-to-speech.
- Added Listen / Pause / Resume / Stop controls inside every lesson.
- Long lessons are split into short speech chunks to avoid browsers stopping on very long utterances.
- Added device voice selection and speed selection.
- Added Nigerian English preference with fallback to available English voices.
- Listening does not require microphone permission.
- Voice commands remain optional and include read lesson, pause voice, resume voice and stop speaking.
- ETHAN Tutor can again use VoiceService.speak.

## Topic coverage
- Added `data/comprehensive-topic-coverage.js`.
- Every course receives a Complete Topic Coverage module based on its subject family.
- Nigeria Mathematics Primary 1 through SS3 gets class-specific comprehensive topic maps.
- Global/specialist Mathematics includes number, arithmetic, algebra, functions, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, matrices/vectors, statistics, probability, finance, discrete mathematics and calculus where appropriate.
- All other subject families receive broad master-topic coverage within ETHAN Learn's defined subject map.

## Curriculum basis
The Nigeria Mathematics progression was checked against official NERDC Basic Education curriculum resources and current revised curriculum portals. The platform's coverage layer is a teaching map, not a substitute for a school/exam-board scheme of work for a specific year.
