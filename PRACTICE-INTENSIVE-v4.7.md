# ETHAN Learn v4.7 — Practice Intensive

Priority: Mathematics, Further/Advanced Mathematics, Accounting, Physics and Chemistry.

Benchmark pattern used: worked examples followed by substantial practice and answer checking. OpenStax Physics, Chemistry and Accounting all use repeated exercise/problem practice. ETHAN's questions are newly generated and are not copied from those sites.

Each detected priority lesson gets a formula/rule reminder, worked example, 15 original practice questions in three levels, checked answers/workings, and a 12/15 mastery target.

Further/Advanced Mathematics gets calculus, integration and matrix-style calculation practice. Mathematics gets arithmetic, percentage, financial arithmetic, algebra, simultaneous equations and quadratic-style practice selected by lesson topic. Accounting gets accounting-equation, profit/margin and reconciliation calculations. Physics gets motion, force, work and power calculations. Chemistry gets mole/mass, concentration and gas-law calculations.

This is a high-volume practice layer. A later content pass can make every individual syllabus topic use its own bespoke question family rather than the broader subject/topic-family generators.
